Privacy Policy — Mimi's Magical World

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Mimi's Magical World is a game for young children (ages 3–6), developed by the creator of Mimi the Kitty.

What data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names, no email addresses.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No photos, contacts, microphone, or camera access.

What stays on the device

Your game progress — coins, purchased outfits, and settings — is saved only in a local file on your own device. It never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Purchases

Optional coin packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google; we never see or store your payment information. Purchases are presented behind a parent gate so a child cannot buy on their own.

Children

Because we collect no data at all, no child's personal information is collected, used, or disclosed, in line with Google Play's Families policy and COPPA.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact email shown on the game's Google Play listing.
