## Full description (EN)
Mimi the Kitty needs your help! Her favorite toys are lost all over the
house, the school, the gym and the beach. Join Mimi in a gentle 3D adventure
made just for little kids (ages 3–6).

Find the toys, beat the clock, and meet Mimi's friends along the way!

FEATURES
• 4 colorful worlds to explore: House, School, Gym and Beach
• Find all the lost toys before time runs out
• Dress Mimi up! Hats, necklaces, scarves and glasses in Mimi's Shop
• Fun mini-games: ABC, Numbers and Sums
• Lulu the Bunny hides in every level — find her for +40 seconds!
• Simple controls made for little hands
• No ads, no data collection, no accounts

Parents: optional coin packs are sold through Google Play and always sit
behind a parent gate, so your child can't buy on their own. The game never
collects or shares any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Made with love for little Mimi fans everywhere!
